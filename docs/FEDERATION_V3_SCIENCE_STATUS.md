# Federation V3 — Cold Nuclear / LENR science status

This repository maps to historical Part VI.2 and related advanced-technology research.

## Federation classification
`SCIENTIFIC_RND / HYPOTHESIS`.

## Priority experiment logic
For Pd–La/Ce/Y/Nd/Gd and related systems, compare the same composition immediately below and above liquidus where feasible to distinguish lattice-specific effects from a broader metallic/electronic-medium hypothesis.

## Required evidence
- exact materials and purity;
- loading/composition state;
- temperature/pressure/current/field history;
- calorimetry calibration and uncertainty;
- blank/control runs;
- isotope/elemental analysis with contamination controls;
- chain-of-custody/sample provenance;
- raw data;
- preregistered success/failure criteria;
- independent reproduction.

## Transmutation claims
Any element/isotope change requires strong analytical controls and independent confirmation. Simulation or post-hoc spectral fit is not transmutation evidence.
